A Love Letter

Hey, random stranger.

These are fragments of a life I was lucky enough to live,
and a world I was lucky enough to see.

These are not photographs of mastery.

Just a love letter to—
the light that found me,
the souls I crossed paths with,
the places that held me for a while,

and all the beautiful things the world has ever given me.
