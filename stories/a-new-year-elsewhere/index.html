December 31, 2025

At the beginning of the year, Shanshan and I traveled to Taizhou, a place I had wanted to visit for a long time. We found our way to the most wonderful seaweed cakes, crisp on the outside, soft and chewy within, with just the right balance of sweet and savory.

A year ago, on New Year’s Eve, we met for the first time. Perhaps that’s why this little journey felt like more than just a trip. We wandered through Taizhou by day, and by night, tucked ourselves into the warmth of our hotel room to watch Stranger Things. On the way back from Ningbo, we finally reached the end of the story, somewhere between one place and the next.

Some memories stay with us in the strangest ways—I wonder if, years from now, when I see this show again, I’ll be carried back to those winter nights, to the quiet joy of watching together, and to the warm, savory sweetness of seaweed cakes.
