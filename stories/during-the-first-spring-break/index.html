March 7, 2017

During my first spring break, I wandered from one little world of wonder to another—from the dazzling streets of Universal Studios to the fairy-tale corners of Disneyland and the lovely creatures at SeaWorld.

There were bright colors, familiar characters, thrilling rides, and so many lovely animals that I could hardly stop taking pictures. For a while, I let myself get lost in these carefully made dreams, where every corner seemed to hold a little surprise and happiness that felt wonderfully easy to find.

Perhaps these places no longer enchant me the way they once did. But looking back, I remember the joy of being there, when everything felt new and magical. Some dreams fade with time, but the happiness we find in them will always stay.
