October 10, 2016

With a camera in hand, I wandered around campus for the first time, stopping for every tree, every patch of sunlight, every little flower along the way. I photographed the open squares, the winding paths, and all the ordinary corners that somehow felt new to me. Back then, the whole campus seemed like a place waiting to be discovered, and I wanted to keep a little piece of everything.

Looking back at those photographs, I realize that not everything has changed. I still stop for the sunlight between the leaves, the flowers growing quietly by the path, the familiar street that look different under a different sky. The world is no longer quite as new as it was back then, but I still find myself looking at it in much the same way—curious about what I might find around the next corner, and always a little delighted by the things I hadn’t noticed before.
