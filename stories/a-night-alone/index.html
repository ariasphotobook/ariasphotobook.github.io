April 3, 2017

Under the fluorescent glow of the fast-food shop near campus, I sat with my little Sony camera, photographing the lights spilling onto the pavement. Cars passed now and then, and strangers drifted in and out through the glass doors. I stayed where I was, turning the dial, trying one night setting after another, finding small things to capture while the evening slowly grew quieter around me.

There were so many nights like this back then. Perhaps I was lonelier than I knew, but I was also learning how to be with myself—to find something worth remembering in an ordinary night, even when there was no one to share it with.
