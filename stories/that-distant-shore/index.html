December 20, 2017

The sea breeze brushed past, carrying the faint smell of salt. I sat on the sand in Santa Monica, watching the waves fold gently onto the shore.

The ocean spoke in its own quiet rhythm, waves rolling in and slipping back, over and over, as if the world had nothing more urgent to do. For a little while, there was nowhere else I needed to be. Just the sea, the birds, the soft sand beneath my feet, and the gentle sound of the waves.

Sometimes, all I want is a quiet place to stay and let the world be.
