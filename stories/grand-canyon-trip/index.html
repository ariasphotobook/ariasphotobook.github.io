December 19, 2016

I remember the Grand Canyon as a place of immense beauty and bitter cold. The wind was sharp against my face, and the vast landscape stretched out before me in layers of red rock and distant blue, beautiful in a way that words could hardly hold.

Now, the details have grown blurry. I can no longer remember exactly what I saw or how the hours passed. Only the cold remains vivid, along with a faint impression of all that beauty, somewhere beyond the reach of memory. Perhaps some places leave us with no clear story to tell, only a feeling that lingers long after the view has faded.
