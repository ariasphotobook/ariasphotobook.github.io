There are still a few things I wish to see before summer fades, and the sea is one of them.

To watch the sunlight scatter across the water, turning the waves into a thousand little pieces of gold. To watch the boats drift between the sea and the sky, and the clouds wander slowly toward the horizon.

I know the summer will pass, as all things do. But for now, I can sit here and watch the water, letting the wind brush past me, grateful that I happened to be here, at this particular moment, before the summer ends.
