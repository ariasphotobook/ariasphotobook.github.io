April 30, 2023

Somewhere between the waking trees and the soft light of spring, Wen and I set out to collect little pieces of the season, with our cameras in hand and an afternoon all to ourselves.

Beneath the trees, we found it in the tender green of new leaves, in branches stretching toward the pale blue sky, and in little plants quietly waking from winter. We stopped often, letting the light guide us from one small wonder to the next, trying to hold on to the things that would soon grow, change, and disappear.

By the time we left, our cameras were filled with leaves, branches, and scattered pieces of sunlight. Perhaps the loveliest things were never the ones we set out to find, but the ones we happened upon along the way.
