April 11, 2017

We climbed the little hill near our college one night, leaving behind the familiar streets and the noise of the day. The path was quiet, the air was cool, and the city slowly unfolded beneath us.

From up there, the buildings seemed smaller, the lights softer, and the world a little less hurried.

We talked about this and that, and sometimes not at all. There was no particular reason to be there, except that we were young, the night was still ours, and the city was beautiful from a distance.

Some moments ask for nothing more than to be lived. Years later, they return quietly, like distant lights on a familiar hillside.
